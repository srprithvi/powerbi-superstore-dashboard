# 📊 Superstore Sales Dashboard | Power BI

## Overview

The Superstore Sales Dashboard is an interactive Power BI report designed to analyze sales performance, customer behavior, product trends, and regional performance using the Sample Superstore dataset.

This project demonstrates data visualization, data modeling, business intelligence reporting, and dashboard design skills using Power BI.

---

## Project Objective

The goal of this dashboard is to help business stakeholders monitor key sales metrics, identify high-performing products and regions, understand customer distribution, and uncover opportunities to improve business performance.

## Key Insights

### Sales Growth
- Sales increased consistently between 2015 and 2018.
- Total sales reached approximately **$0.72M** in 2018, making it the strongest performing year.

### Category Performance
- Technology generated the highest total sales (**$0.83M**).
- Furniture followed with approximately **$0.73M**.
- Office Supplies contributed approximately **$0.71M**.

### Regional Performance
- West region generated the highest sales and customer count.
- South region produced the lowest sales among the four regions.

### Customer Segments
- Consumer segment contributed over **50%** of total sales.
- Corporate segment accounted for approximately **30%**.
- Home Office segment contributed approximately **19%**.

### Geographic Insights
- New York City generated the highest sales among all cities.
- Los Angeles and Seattle were also top-performing markets.

### Product Performance
- Canon imageCLASS 2200 Advanced Copier was the highest-selling product.
- Technology products dominated the list of top-performing items.

### Shipping Analysis
- Average shipping time remained consistent across categories at approximately 4 days.
